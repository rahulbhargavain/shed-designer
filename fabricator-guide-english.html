4. Step-by-Step Build Schedule
1
Marking and Cutting

Cut all angles and plates per the cut list. Mark and pre-drill all M12 and M16 bolt holes before welding to ensure accuracy.

2
Welding Vertical Corner Members

Weld 2 pairs of corner verticals back-to-back (TL and BR corners only) using a continuous/intermittent fillet weld (≥ 5 mm). TR and BL corners, and the central stringers, must remain as single angles.

3
Weld Vertical Stiffeners to Yokes

Fit the two 75×5 mm vertical flat bars down the centerline of the 300 mm faces and weld them securely at each yoke intersection. This creates a vertical beam support structure.

4
Assemble the 450 mm Face Panels

Sandwich-bolt the 20-inch (508 mm) plywood sheathing panels to the central stringers and double corner angles. Spacing of M12 bolts should be ≤ 250 mm.

5
Erect Panels and Clamp Yokes (Base to Top)

Erect the panels, verify plumb on both axes, and secure with diagonal kicker braces. Fit yokes starting from the ground (0 mm) up to 2438 mm. Use M16 Grade 8.8 bolts on Yokes 1 to 5.

6
Pre-Pour Checks and Concreting

Verify all fasteners are tight and seams are sealed. Pour M25 concrete in lifts of no more than 500 mm, compacting with a vibrator between each lift.
